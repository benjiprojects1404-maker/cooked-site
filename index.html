How the kitchen works

No presale, no team bag, no secret recipe. Three steps, all run by the contract.

  1. Cook on the curve

    Buy COOKED with BDAG. Each buy raises the price a little and each sell lowers it. 800 million COOKED are on the curve, and the last one costs 16 times the first.

  2. The oven hits 100%

    When all 800 million are sold, the curve closes. Nobody can buy or sell on it any more.

  3. Served on Reef

    Anyone presses Serve it. The BDAG raised and the last 200 million COOKED go into a Reef pool at the curve's final price, and the pool tokens are burned so that starting liquidity stays forever.

Questions

Can the liquidity be pulled? The starting liquidity was burned when the oven was served, so nobody can ever withdraw it, including me. Anyone can also add their own liquidity to the Reef pool and gets LP tokens for it. Those belong to them and can be removed at any time. Removing your own liquidity only returns your share; it can't take anyone else's funds, and other providers keep their percentage. If someone removes a lot, the pool gets thinner and trades move the price more. That risk applies to every pool.

Whose is the large LP position that was in the pool? It was mine. On 9 Oct 2026 I added liquidity from my wallet 0xcA83…99B5, mistakenly thinking the oven would reopen. It doesn't. I don't want it, so I burned all of those LP tokens: see the burn transaction. The pool's starting liquidity and my added liquidity are both now at the burn address.

If I add liquidity, can I get it back? Yes, you can remove it whenever you like and receive your share of the BDAG and COOKED in the pool at the current price. That share can be worth more or less than what you put in, because the price moves with trades.

Chef's warning

This is a meme. It can go to zero. Only cook with what you'd happily burn.

  • Nobody's running the kitchen. There's no owner, no team allocation, no tax and no blacklist, and nothing in the contract can be changed.
  • The price moves fast both ways. Buying late on the curve costs up to 16 times more than buying early, and after it's served on Reef the price is whatever the market says.
  • Bots may try to buy in the first seconds after the oven opens. The curve treats them like everyone else, but they're quick.
  • The contracts are tested and had an AI-assisted security review (no critical or high issues found), but they are not independently audited, and BlockDAG is an early-stage chain.
  • Check that buying tokens like this is legal where you live. This page isn't financial advice.

How it works

One transaction, one contract, nothing hidden.

1. Order

Pick a name, a ticker and a supply, how much goes into the pool and how much BDAG to pair it with. That sets the starting price, shown before you confirm.

2. Cook

One transaction makes your token, opens its TOKEN/BDAG pool on Reef and burns the pool's LP. The rest of the supply comes to you; the 5,000 BDAG fee goes to Reef's treasury.

3. Serve

It's trading on Reef straight away, with liquidity nobody can pull. Share the safety report. You're free to list it anywhere else later too.

Unaudited. The Kitchen contract has tests and an internal review, but no independent audit. Source on GitHub.

How to earn it

After 7 days: 15 REEF

  • The pool still has at least 10,000 BDAG of liquidity on Reef
  • At least 10 holders besides you

After 14 days: +15 REEF

  • At least 25 holders besides you
  • At least 50,000 BDAG traded in its first 14 days
  • Launches on BlockDAG through the Kitchen only, made between 20 Nov and 31 Dec 2026 (Bangkok time), plus FLASH as an early supporter. One reward per wallet: your first launch that qualifies. Launches by Reef's own team don't count.
  • Holders don't count the pool, the burn address, the Kitchen or you. Liquidity is the pool's total value in BDAG, as Reef shows it.
  • REEF is paid to the wallet that cooked the token, by hand, once a week, from Reef's marketing wallet. Every payment is listed below with its transaction.
  • Wash trading, buying with your own wallets or anything else that games the rules doesn't qualify. Reef has the final say on payouts.
  • REEF is a reward for a launch people use, not an investment. Its value moves with the market. Not for US/UK persons.
